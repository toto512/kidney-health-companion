The short version

  • You give us health details so the app can show them back to you. We keep them for you, not for anyone else.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use your data to train AI.
  • Your lab files are read on your own phone or computer. They are not sent to any outside reading or AI service.
  • You can export your blood test results and delete your account and all your data at any time.
  • This app gives information only. It is not medical advice. If it is an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Photos and files

  • Blood test files and photos are read on your device (text reading runs inside the app). Nothing is sent to a cloud reading or AI service.
  • If you choose to save the original file, it goes into a private storage area that only your account can open.
  • Blood pressure pictures and plate photos are shown on your screen only. They are not uploaded or saved.
  • Camera access is used only when you tap a camera button.

How we use it

  • To run the features you use.

  • To keep the app safe, for example to block automated sign-up attempts.

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not use it for ads. We do not share it with insurers or employers.

On your device

The app keeps small things on your device: your sign-in session, text size, language, units, and short hints you dismissed. If you switch it on, it can also keep a copy of "My people" on the device so you can call without internet. You can switch that off, and the copy is removed. You are signed out after 15 minutes of no use.

How long we keep it

We keep your information until you delete it or delete your account. In the app you can delete a blood test and its file, or choose Delete my account. Deleting your account removes your sign-in, your data, and your stored files. Copies in provider backups, if any, are removed on their normal schedule. Canadian privacy law

We handle personal information under Canada's federal privacy law (PIPEDA). Health information is sensitive, so we ask for your clear consent when you create your account and add information. If you live in Quebec, Quebec's Law 25 also gives you rights, including to access, correct, and delete your information, and to know when it is stored outside Quebec. If we find a breach of our safeguards that creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will tell you and the privacy regulator as the law requires. You may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

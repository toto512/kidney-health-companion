Nephrology Principles for CKD Stage 4 (eGFR 15–29 mL/min)

Primary clinical imperatives: Restrict Potassium (<2,000 mg/day) to prevent fatal arrhythmias; Restrict Inorganic Phosphorus (avoid preservatives labeled "PHOS"); Moderate Protein (0.6–0.8 g/kg/day) to avoid uremic accumulation; and Limit Sodium (<1,500–2,000 mg/day) to control extracellular volume overload and progressive glomerulosclerosis.

Rheumatology Principles for Hyperuricemia & Gout

Gout is characterized by monosodium urate crystal deposition in joints and kidneys (urate nephropathy). The biochemical focus is preventing exogenous purine loading (organ meats, small pelagic fish, beer) and adenosine triphosphate (ATP) depletion from high-fructose corn syrup. Note: Low-fat dairy and anthocyanin-rich cherries enhance urate clearance.

Vascular & Nephrologic Control of Blood Pressure

Hypertension drives glomerular sclerosis and renovascular disease. Reducing sodium chloride intake (<2,000 mg/day) restores the endothelium and limits plasma volume expansion. Important Clinical Adjustment: The standard DASH diet encourages high-potassium foods (spinach, bananas), but CKD Stage 4 patients cannot clear potassium and must substitute non-potassium vasodilators such as dietary nitrates (beets in moderation) or allicin (garlic).

Nephrology & Rheumatology Notice

Why this triad is uniquely perilous: Chronic Kidney Disease Stage 4 creates a metabolic barrier where organs can no longer excrete potassium, inorganic phosphate, or nitrogenous compounds.

  • Gout & CKD: Failing kidneys cannot excrete uric acid, precipitating severe gout and urate stone formation. However, allopurinol/febuxostat must be dosed according to GFR, and diet must avoid purines without relying excessively on high-potassium beans.
  • Hypertension & CKD: High pressure destroys surviving nephrons. Yet, the classic high-potassium DASH remedy must be curtailed to prevent serum potassium exceeding 5.5 mEq/L.
  • Always Consult: Blood chemistry labs (BUN, Creatinine, K+, PO4, Uric Acid, BP) dictate your exact personalized limits.
